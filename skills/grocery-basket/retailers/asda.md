## Searching and adding

- A privacy banner covers the page on first visit. Choose **Reject All**.
- **Search is literal.** "loose ginger" returned a nail varnish called "Lose Your Lingerie"; "ginger" returned the right products. Search with the plain ingredient name and drop words such as "loose" or "fresh".
- Results load a second or two after the page; wait before reading them.

## Loose produce

Loose items are sold by count at a per-item price, with the per-kg price beside it: "ASDA Loose Red Pepper £0.70 (70.0p/each)", "ASDA Ginger £0.80 (£9.41/kg)". No weight toggle was visible. Add by count.

## Prices and units

Cards show the pack size above the name ("3pk", "480g", "each"), then the price and unit price in brackets. Offers show "was £0.97 actual price £0.80".

## Minimum basket and charges

From Asda's "Shopping online" help page: the minimum spend is £40 for delivery and £25 for Click & Collect. Below it, Asda adds a £3 minimum-basket charge. Delivery charges vary by slot (reported at £1 to £7); a Delivery Pass removes them. A customer review says the running total in the header can include fees, so check the basket page for the goods total before saying the minimum is met.
