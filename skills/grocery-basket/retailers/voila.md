# Voilà (voila.ca)

Tested live: no (read-only study, 10 Nov 2026)

Voilà is Sobeys' online grocery, run on the Ocado platform (the same as Ocado and Morrisons in the UK). It delivers from Sobeys-family stores; which stores and areas depend on the address. In Quebec, IGA runs its own Voilà service.

## Searching and adding

- Product names carry the count or weight: "Zucchini Squash Green 2 Count", "Chinese Eggplant 500 g".
- The page asks the user to choose a location (postal code). Use what the user has set; ask before changing it.

## Loose produce

Loose produce is sold by count at a typical weight: "Zucchini Squash 1 Count — 0.3kg typically ($7.69 per kilogram), Price $2.31 typically". Add by count; the final charge depends on the weight picked. Packs show a per-item price: "2 per pack ($2.00 per item), $3.99".

## Prices and units

Metric: per kilogram for produce; per item for packs.

## Minimum basket and charges

The header showed "Minimum: $35.00", matching Voilà's own pages. Delivery fees depend on the slot (a 2026 guide reports $6 to $16) and are shown at checkout. A Voilà Delivery Pass is offered.

## Substitutions

From Voilà's pages: if an item is out of stock at picking time, Voilà suggests a similar item; the customer can refuse substitutes at the door and is not charged for them.
