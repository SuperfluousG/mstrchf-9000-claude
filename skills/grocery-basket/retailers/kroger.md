Kroger runs the same site under other banners, each on its own domain: Ralphs, Fred Meyer, King Soopers, Smith's, Fry's, QFC, Dillons, Mariano's, City Market and others. Use this guide on whichever banner the user is signed in to; swap the domain in the search URL.

## Searching and adding

- A consent notice ("Improving Your Experience") appears over the results with only a close button. Results can be read underneath it.
- Search results include sponsored cards (a bag of spinach topped the "zucchini" results).
- Signed out, every Add button reads "Sign in to add … to your cart". In the user's signed-in tab, find the Add button by the product name the same way.
- The header shows the store and how the user shops ("Pickup at <store>"). Use what is set; ask before changing it.

## Loose produce

Loose produce shows an estimated price per item and a per-pound price: "Zucchini — about $0.90 each, $1.79/lb". Add by count. For a weight plan, divide ($1.79/lb ÷ $0.90 each ≈ 2 per lb).

## Minimum basket and charges

From Kroger's delivery pages and 2026 guides: a $35 order minimum is cited for delivery; a promotion waived delivery fees on orders of $50 or more until 7 July 2026, where available. Non-member delivery fees are reported at $6.95 to $9.95 plus a possible service fee. A Boost membership gives free delivery on orders over $35. Read the actual fees in the cart.

## Substitutions

A consumer guide describes a control above the item list in the cart to allow substitutions for out-of-stock items. Kroger banner terms say the customer is told about substitutions at pickup or delivery and can reject them then. Leave the user's setting as it is.
