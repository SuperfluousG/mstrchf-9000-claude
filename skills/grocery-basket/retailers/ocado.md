## Searching and adding

- Results can show the same product twice (a promoted copy and the normal one). Add it once.
- Ocado, Morrisons and Voilà run on the same Ocado platform. Their pages share card layout and label style.

## Loose produce

Ocado sells fresh produce in fixed packs, priced per item or per kilo: "Ocado Red Peppers, 3 per pack, 70.0p per item, £2.10", "Ocado Root Ginger 125g, £13.20 per kilo, £1.65". No loose-by-weight items were seen. Choose the pack closest to the plan.

## Prices and units

Cards show "Ordinarily <unit price> (<unit price>) Price £x.xx" and the pack size ("3 per pack", "250g").

## Minimum basket and charges

From Ocado's terms and 2026 guides: the minimum order is shown next to the trolley icon (reported as £40). Delivery charges depend on the order value, date and time (reported £2.99 to £6.99 under £75, sometimes free above). A small-order charge may apply below a level above the minimum. Smart Pass may remove delivery charges. Read the actual figures in the trolley.

## Substitutions

Not confirmed. Customer reports say substitutions cannot be switched off in advance and can be rejected at the door. If a rejected substitute could take the order under the minimum, mention it.
