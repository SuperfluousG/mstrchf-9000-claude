# Amazon Fresh (amazon.com)

Tested live: no (read-only study, 10 Nov 2026: blocked on the first page)

## Do not drive this site

The first page loaded in the study (`https://www.amazon.com/s?k=zucchini&i=amazonfresh`) returned an "Access restricted" page. It said continued access by an unauthorised AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use, which Amazon customers have agreed to. The study stopped there and touched nothing else on amazon.com.

So for Amazon Fresh:

1. Before opening any Amazon page, tell the user that Amazon's Conditions of Use bar unauthorised AI agents, so you won't fill the basket for them.
2. Offer the list in a form that is quick to shop by hand: one line per item with the search phrase, the packs to buy and the meals it is for, in aisle order.
3. If the user still has an Amazon tab open and asks you to carry on, do not. If an "Access restricted" page appears at any point, stop at once and do not press Continue.

## What is known without the site

From 2026 news reports, not from Amazon pages:

- Prime members get free Amazon Fresh delivery on orders over $100 (raised to $150 in February 2025, later lowered to $100). Below that the fee is $6.95 for $50 to $100 and $9.95 under $50. Non-Prime fees are $7.95 to $13.95, by basket size and window.
- Amazon's grocery own brands include Amazon Fresh, Amazon Grocery and Whole Foods Market 365. Not checked on the site.

## Not seen

Everything on the site: search, product cards, Add buttons, loose produce, the cart, substitutions and the delivery-window flow.
