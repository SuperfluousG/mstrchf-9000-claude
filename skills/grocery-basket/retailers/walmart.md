## Searching and adding

- Each card can carry three buttons with the product name: `Sign in to add to Favorites list, <name>`, `Add to cart - <name>` and `Subscribe <name>`. Use only Add to cart. **Never press Subscribe**: it sets up repeat orders.
- Search mixes store groceries with shipped and marketplace items (for "zucchini": frozen spirals, fresh zucchini, and a 12-per-case catering pack). Choose items that offer Pickup or Delivery from the user's store; skip "Shipping"-only and case-pack items.
- The header shows the user's store and how they shop ("How do you want your items?": Shipping, Pickup, Delivery). Use what is set; ask before changing it.

## Loose produce

Loose produce is sold by count with an estimated price: "Fresh Zucchini, Each — $0.71 each (est.), $1.42/lb, Final cost by weight". Add by count; the final charge depends on the weight picked. For a weight plan ("1 lb"), divide by the estimate ($1.42/lb ÷ $0.71 each ≈ 2 per lb).

## Prices and units

Cards show the price and a unit price in cents per ounce ("$3.86, 32.2 ¢/oz"), or "each (est.)" and "$/lb" for produce.

## Minimum basket and charges

From 2026 third-party guides (not a Walmart page): store delivery has a $35 minimum on the pre-tax total of eligible items. Below it, a fee is added (reported $6.99) or standard delivery may be unavailable. Non-members pay a per-order delivery fee (reported $7.95 to $9.95); Walmart+ removes it on orders over $35. Express (faster) delivery costs extra. Read the actual fees in the cart.

Not confirmed. Walmart's substitution preferences are set per item or per order in the cart or at checkout; leave them as the user has them.
