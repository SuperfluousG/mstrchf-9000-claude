## Searching and adding

- Morrisons runs on the Ocado platform: the card layout matches Ocado and Voilà.
- The same product can appear twice with almost identical labels (for example `Add Morrisons Loose Red Pepper to basket` and the same label with a trailing space). The copies differ by shelf-life badge: "LIFE 1d+" and "LIFE 2d+" mean typical days of life after delivery. Prefer the longer life and add only one of them.
- Branded items on offer can rank above own-brand produce. Check the name before adding.

## Loose produce

Loose items are sold by count: "Morrisons Loose Red Pepper, 80.0p/item, £0.80". Add by count.

## Prices and units

Cards show "Ordinarily <unit price> Price £x.xx" and the pack size. Offers show "Now £2.75, Was £4".

## Minimum basket and charges

Sources disagree. A Morrisons help page gives a £25 minimum for standard home delivery; an undated listing reports a £3 surcharge on orders under £40. Morrisons on Amazon and Morrisons Now (one-hour delivery) are separate services with their own minimums. Read the actual charge on the basket page.

## Substitutions

Morrisons' terms let the customer ask for a refund on an unwanted substitute after delivery. The in-order substitution setting was not seen.
