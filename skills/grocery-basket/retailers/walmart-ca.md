## Searching and adding

- Each card also has `Sign in to add to Favourites list, <name>`. Use only Add to cart.
- Product names carry the selling unit: "Zucchini, Green, Sold in singles, 0.09 - 0.22 KG", "Zucchini, Organic, Tray of 2 counts".
- Results can include marketplace items ("Sold By" filter). Skip anything not sold by Walmart for pickup or delivery.

## Loose produce

Loose produce is sold in singles with a weight range and an average price: "Sold in singles, 0.09 - 0.22 KG — current price 66¢, avg price 42.0 ¢/100g, Final cost by weight". Add by count. For a weight plan, use the middle of the range (about 0.15 kg each here).

## Prices and units

Metric: cents per 100 g, and kg for produce.

## Minimum basket and charges

From walmart.ca's grocery page and Help Centre: free pickup and delivery need a $35 minimum (before taxes and fees). Same Day Delivery costs $8.97 per order, and orders under $35 pay a $7.97 small-order fee (Help Centre; may be dated). Pickup is free except PenguinPickUp and Express Grocery Pickup. Fees are shown at checkout.

## Substitutions

From walmart.ca: substitutions are opt-in at checkout, for the whole order or for selected items. Walmart picks an equal or better item at no extra charge. Leave the user's choice as it is.
